# Versioned legal acceptance for new and existing accounts

Accepted on 2026-11-03. Implementation is authorized by the approved
[legal-pages design](../legal-pages-design.md).

Track Atlas requires explicit terms acceptance and a separate privacy-policy
acknowledgment, recording document versions, timestamp, and language while
preserving the corresponding historical documents. Privacy acknowledgment is
not consent to optional processing. Existing accounts, including active sessions,
must complete the same step before using authenticated features: this accepts a
one-time interruption instead of inferring agreement from prior registration or
continued use. Material revisions require renewed acceptance or acknowledgment;
typo-only corrections do not interrupt users. Legal pages, privacy-request
information, and logout remain accessible before completion.
